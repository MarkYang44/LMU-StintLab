# STX branding

The owner supplied the monochrome and color STINTRIX references and requested these project-specific variants. Edits used the built-in image generation tool. Both source images stay local to the repository; build-time code only resizes and encodes them into PNG/ICO resources. Original reference files are untouched.

- `src/branding/stx-app.png`: complete STX + STINTRIX logo, white on black.
- `src/branding/stx-menu.png`: yellow-green and white logo, black menu banner.
- Generated Windows icon sizes: 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixels. The complete mark is retained at every size.

## App icon edit prompt

Edit the supplied monochrome STX / STINTRIX logo by inverting its colors only: the original white background becomes opaque pure black (#000000), and all original black artwork becomes white (#FFFFFF). Preserve the full original composition, connected angular STX racing monogram above, and the entire small STINTRIX wordmark below, spelled S T I N T R I X. The small STINTRIX text is mandatory; do not remove or omit it. Preserve its stylized typography, negative spaces and relative proportions. Keep a square canvas and the complete two-level logo centered with generous black margins as in the supplied reference. Faithfully retain the original geometry and layout; no new design, no added symbols, no border, no gradients, no textures, no glow, no shadows. Flat high-contrast white-on-black application branding, including BOTH the large STX monogram AND the lower STINTRIX text.

## Menu edit prompt

Edit the supplied STX STINTRIX yellow-green/white logo on black into a wide desktop-menu branding banner. Keep exactly the original STX monogram, its angular racing strokes, the thin yellow-green sweep through the S, the white ST strokes and yellow-green top and X strokes. Preserve the exact stylized word STINTRIX below, spelled S T I N T R I X, with the same yellow-green accents on the final X. Only remove excess empty margins: arrange the original mark above the original wordmark, centered as in the input, in a wide 2:1 rectangular composition with small, even black margins. Use a pure black opaque background, bright yellow-green (#E6FF00) accents and white lettering. No extra text, no character icon, no framing border, no redesign of the monogram, no gradients, no texture, no glow. Crisp polished flat artwork suitable for the LMU-Stintrix menu.
